Two things to know before routing

  • Ping Ryan Creasia internally before assigning any of his tickets.
  • DIT does not have Hilco / Hilsinger M365 admin credentials.

Do not assign any of Ryan's tickets without pinging him first — confirm he handles it before you route it.

Without M365 admin access, any ticket requiring Hilco/Hilsinger tenant-level admin actions cannot be completed by DIT directly — flag these for escalation.

If unsure on routing, check the IT Glue doc or ask escalations.

This app does not track ticket status — keep all notes and updates in ConnectWise and Jira.

📋 Routing Process — Follow this order every time you pass a ticket
If DIT handles it, just work it like a normal ticket. If you're routing it to someone else, follow these steps:
  1. Escalate first. Put the ticket in escalations and get approval before doing anything. Approvers: Taylor Wanamaker · Andrew Minear · John Tuley · Thomas Traver · Kyle
  2. Assign in Jira once you have approval.
  3. Leave an internal note in the Jira ticket. Write what you saw in IT Glue that made you route it, who you assigned it to, and your initials at the bottom.
    "Item pricing - passing to Paula
    -KS"
  4. Complete the ticket in ConnectWise.
1 Escalate & get approval → 2 Assign in Jira → 3 Internal note + initials in Jira → 4 Complete in CW
